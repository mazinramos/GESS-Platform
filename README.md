<h2>Overview</h2>



<p>

GESS Platform is an educational system designed to provide a structured digital environment for students and administrators.

</p>



<p>

The platform focuses on organizing educational content and providing a simple experience for accessing and managing learning resources.

</p>



<h2>Core Areas</h2>



<h3>Student Experience</h3>



<p>

A dedicated environment for students to access the available educational content and platform resources.

</p>



<h3>Administration</h3>



<p>

Administrative functionality for managing the platform and its educational content.

</p>



<h2>Project Goals</h2>



<ul>

<li>Organize educational resources in one platform</li>

<li>Provide a structured student experience</li>

<li>Support administrative management</li>

<li>Create a foundation for future educational features</li>

</ul>
